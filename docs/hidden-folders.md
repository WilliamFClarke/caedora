# Hidden folders

Some folders in a vault are not part of day to day reading, such as `.github`
with its workflow code, or a `research` folder that is only opened now and then.
They can be hidden from the sidebar.

- Right click a folder and choose Hide folder. Choose Show folder to bring it
  back.
- Folders whose name starts with a dot, such as `.github`, are hidden without
  being asked. Show folder keeps one visible.
- When any folder is hidden, a Show hidden folders switch appears at the bottom
  of the sidebar. With it on, hidden folders are listed dimmed so their notes can
  still be opened.
- Hidden folders stay in full text search and the link map. To leave them out
  of both, turn off Search hidden folders in Settings, General.

Hiding is a display choice for this browser only. It is stored per vault in
IndexedDB next to folder colours and icons, and it never changes, moves or
renames anything in the vault, so other tools and other devices see every
folder as before.
